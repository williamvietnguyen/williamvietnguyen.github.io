## November 15, 2026

### Mdina

Mdina, the silent city, is a fortified medieval city. There is a few hundred inhabitants. I went early in the morning. Within an hour or so, tourist groups were filling up the area. But despite that, the city was still quiet. It's very small area. Maze-like passages that you can walk through. Absolutely gorgeous location.

The catacombs had a weird smell to it. The day was quite humid as well. It was warm in the catacombs.

You might also be thinking from the images, that it is well lit down there. It's not. My phone camera made the images 10 times brighter. It was very dark in the catacombs.

Also don't go if you're not interested in seeing human remains. People's bones are on display in the catacombs.
